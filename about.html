about

glassgarden is the ambient project of Carlos, based in Brazil.

The music is generative. Instead of writing every note, I set up a few rules and let small instruments respond to each other. One keeps a slow pulse, another decides when to speak, a third listens and answers. My part is mostly listening, and nudging when something interesting starts to happen.

Nothing is arranged on a timeline. Some pieces are played live on small instruments, so each recording is a single take of something that never quite repeats. Others are written as code and keep running wherever you open them, like terrarium in your browser.

The cc in glassgarden.cc is for control change, the MIDI message that turns a knob on something already playing. That's most of my part: not writing the notes, just a hand on the knob.

approach

  • rules, not scores
  • live, or left to run
  • no two runs alike
  • slow and unhurried
